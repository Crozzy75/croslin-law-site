About Croslin Law

Your priorities, our commitment

Who we are

Croslin Law serves individuals and families across Tulsa and Green Country. Whether the question is custody, a divorce that involves a home, a retirement account, or a family business, or planning to protect what you’ve built for the next generation, we help you see your options clearly before you decide. We understand that few things matter more to people than their freedom, their children, and their livelihoods, and that understanding drives how we practice.

No two cases are alike. Our goal is to make sure clients understand the possible outcomes in their case so they can make informed decisions about how to proceed. We then carry out that plan with careful preparation.

How we work

  • Plain language. The law isn’t easy, so we explain your options in terms you can understand, without jargon.
  • Honest advice. You don’t need someone who only tells you what you want to hear. You need an advocate. We help you understand the possible outcomes, and the decision about how to proceed is yours.
  • Efficiency. We know legal help is a real expense. We use modern tools, such as file management and document drafting software, to cut the time spent on routine work, which helps keep costs down.
  • Respect for your situation. People often come to us in the hardest moments of their lives. We treat every client with respect and dignity.

Our philosophy

When it comes to your personal life, you want to know what will happen next and be in control. Croslin Law gets that. Our goal is to inform clients of the outcomes that are possible and have them make informed decisions, so that they can choose the outcomes that are best for them. Our client-first approach puts the major decisions in your hands, and we handle the legalese that quickly becomes overwhelming.

Where we practice

Our office is located at 1234 ABC Blvd, Tulsa, OK. We serve Tulsa and Green Country, including Tulsa, Creek, Osage, Rogers, Wagoner, Washington, Okmulgee, Muskogee, Pawnee, Mayes, Nowata, Cherokee, and Kay counties.
