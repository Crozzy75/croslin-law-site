About Brayden

Brayden Croslin is the founding attorney of Croslin Law, PLLC, and his practice is focused on family law. He has advocated for clients at trial in guardianships, adoptions, emergency custody hearings, contempt defenses, child support modifications, and custody modifications.

Brayden’s work draws on trial advocacy, motion practice, and alternative dispute resolution, along with mentorship from experienced family law attorneys and ongoing continuing legal education in trial advocacy and family law.

Outside the office, Brayden spends time with his spouse, three children, and two dogs. He loves taking his kids to the arcade. Brayden is a major fan of OU Football and Thunder Basketball and tries to watch a game any opportunity he can.

Focus areas

Brayden’s family law practice includes divorce, custody and visitation, child support, paternity, guardianship, adoption, grandparent visitation, gestational surrogacy agreements, and prenuptial agreements. He has expanded his services to include wills and trusts, probate, asset protection, criminal defense, expungements, protective orders, and appeals.

Brayden practices in the Tulsa area and across Green Country, appearing in Tulsa, Creek, Osage, Rogers, Wagoner, Washington, Okmulgee, Muskogee, Pawnee, Mayes, Nowata, Cherokee, and Kay Counties.
