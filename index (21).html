Privacy policy

Information we collect

We collect information about visitors in three ways: directly from you, automatically through our web server logs, and through cookies and similar technologies.

Information you give us. If you contact us through the website, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry. Please do not include confidential or sensitive details in your message.

Information collected automatically. Our website host keeps server logs that may record technical information about your visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you view.

Fonts. Our site loads fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website sends to your browser and that are stored on your device. We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages they view and how they find us, so that we can improve it.

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google. It uses cookies to collect information that your browser sends with each page request, such as your IP address, the pages you visit, how long you stay, your device and browser type, and your approximate location. We use this information to understand website trends. Google handles it under its own privacy policy.

If we run online advertising, such as Google Ads, our advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to measure how well our ads perform. We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers.

Your choices. Most browsers accept cookies by default, but you can set yours to notify you when a cookie is sent or to block cookies. If you block them, some parts of this site and others may not work properly. For instructions, see All About Cookies. To opt out of Google Analytics, you can install Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. To manage how Google uses information for advertising, visit Google’s Ad Settings, or visit aboutads.info/choices for opt-outs from participating advertisers.

By using this website, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

Confidentiality and security

Please do not send confidential or sensitive information through this website. Contacting us through the site, including the contact form, does not create an attorney-client relationship. Details about your matter are best shared in a private consultation.

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from accidental loss and from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee that those measures will never be defeated.

We do not sell, rent, or lease mailing lists or other user data, and we do not share your personal information with unaffiliated parties except:

  • with service providers, such as our website host and email provider, that handle it on our behalf;
  • with your consent, if we are unable to help with your matter and you ask us to refer you to another attorney or firm; and
  • as required by law, to protect our rights or property, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the firm, to the extent the rules governing lawyers permit.
